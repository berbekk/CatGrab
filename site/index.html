Six kinds of action

Every sector does one thing — you decide which.

Launch an app, open a link, press a shortcut for you, run a macOS action or paste a snippet. Point at the ring to see what each kind does.

Hotkey or trackpad

Summon it the way your hands already are.

Give every menu its own key combination — even a bare fn press — or a light tap with three, four or five fingers. The ring opens under the pointer, wherever it is.

  • Any combination. ⌃⌥Space, ⌘Tab, a single F-key or just fn.
  • Tap, don't click. macOS swipes and pinches keep working.
  • One trigger, one menu. Each menu remembers its own.

Active apps

⌘Tab, but round.

A built-in ring of everything that's running. Flick to an app and CatGrab brings it forward — on its own Space, with minimized windows back from the Dock.

  • Muscle memory friendly. Keep ⌘ held and press Tab to step around the ring.
  • Your order. The last app you used sits first; hide the ones you never switch to.

App commands

The commands of the app in front — on one hotkey.

Out of the box: the halves of the screen, each on its own side of the ring, plus fill, center, full screen and quit. Give an app its own set from its menu bar, and the same hotkey shows Figma's commands in Figma and Xcode's in Xcode.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

No account, no installer, nothing running in the background except the cat in your menu bar.

  1. Download CatGrab.dmg

    From the latest release on GitHub. It's about 4 MB.

  2. Drag it into Applications

    Then open CatGrab from there, like any other app.

  3. Allow two permissions

    Accessibility presses keys and moves windows; Input Monitoring hears your hotkeys.

The first launch needs one confirmation

CatGrab isn't notarized by Apple — that takes a paid developer account — so macOS asks you to confirm it once.

  • macOS 15 and newer: System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down → Open Anyway.
  • macOS 13–14: Control-click CatGrab in Applications → Open.

Prefer Terminal?

This removes the “downloaded from the internet” mark from CatGrab only:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/CatGrab.app
